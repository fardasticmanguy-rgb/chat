Unit 4 Review: Cells and Energy

Photosynthesis

  • Happens in the chloroplasts of plant cells
  • 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
  • Light reactions in the thylakoid membrane make ATP and NADPH
  • Calvin cycle in the stroma uses them to fix carbon

Cellular respiration

  • Glycolysis in the cytoplasm: glucose → 2 pyruvate (net 2 ATP)
  • Krebs cycle in the mitochondrial matrix
  • Electron transport chain makes most of the ATP (about 34)
  • Without oxygen: fermentation (lactic acid or alcohol)

Enzymes

  • Lower activation energy, are not used up
  • Shape of the active site matters (lock and key)
  • Too much heat or wrong pH can denature them
